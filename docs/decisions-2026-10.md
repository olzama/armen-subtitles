- **Meme spans re-mapped for all models with one rule** (Olga's call: "trim and rescore"). The interactive mapping had trimmed some conditions (mostly *given*) to the meme and left others with the surrounding subtitle dialogue, which the judge penalizes. `code/extract_meme_spans.py` maps each meme by time code: if the meme fills its source entries ("exact"), the translation of exactly those entries is taken, deterministically; if the entries also hold other dialogue ("trim"), GPT-6 Sol (reasoning low) trims the translation of those entries only; if the entries do not contain the meme ("search", e.g. reordered or wrongly numbered), it searches ±3 entries. An LLM-only version (GPT-5.4) was tried first and rejected: it dropped or added parts of memes whose usual citation differs in word order from the subtitles. Spans that are not verbatim substrings of the translation are not applied (logged in `mapping/span_extraction.json`). Old mappings kept as `<model>.pre-span.json`, old eval dirs as `mqm-memes.pre-span`.
