# Meme Translation Guide: *The Diamond Arm* (Бриллиантовая рука, 1969) → English

## Rules for the whole film

1. **Repeated lines must use the same English wording every time.** Many of these memes depend on recurrence: the cast story, *без шума и пыли*, *цигель*, "city of contrasts". If the wording drifts, the callback is lost.
2. **Each character's register is part of the joke.**
   - Lyolik speaks with southern/Odessa features (*у* for *в*, *усё*, *сымем*).
   - Gesha uses gentlemanly clichés.
   - Officials speak in propaganda language.
   - Use a recognisable English equivalent for each, but don't map anyone onto a specific real dialect. For Lyolik, Yiddish-inflected English syntax ("You should live on one salary!") is the closest natural fit for his Odessa voice.
3. **The fake proverbs must sound like real proverbs.** Keep them short, rhythmic and pompous, and keep a rhyme where the Russian has one.

## Lyolik's "As our dear chief says…"

These are fake folk wisdom. They should sound quotable and slightly off.

- **36:** "The main thing is this here realism." The censored word *socialist* left an awkward gap, and that gap is part of the joke, so keep the filler.
- **265–267:** "If a man's an idiot, it's for the long haul."
- **738–740:** "On someone else's dime, the teetotaller and the ulcer case both have a fine time." The rhyme matters more than literal accuracy.
- **1192–1193:** "There's no husband who hasn't dreamed, if only for an hour, of being a bachelor."
- **1365, 1407:** "Strike while the iron's at the till." This fuses "strike while the iron is hot" with the shop sign "check your change at the till". English can make the same splice.

## Loose ends

- **Unverified claims (marked † in the findings):** the *цигель* origin, the till-sign splice, the Kolyma reading, the *сядем* pun, and the Kozlodoev etymology. All are well known but have no source cited.
- **"Не виноватая я":** the Click or Die source frames it as a reference to a forgotten *film* (probably the 1960 *Resurrection* adaptation), not directly to Tolstoy's novel. Check this before citing.
- **Subtitle-file typos:** *гланое*, *Казладоев*, *учавствую*, *Житомере*. These matter if the file is used as a reference.
- **Paper:** "Operation Fowl", "strike while the iron's at the till" and the untranslatable-pun note are good paper-example candidates. If you want them in the paper, they go in docs/paper-examples.md first.
